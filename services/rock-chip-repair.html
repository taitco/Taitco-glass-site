Why Repair Instead of Replace?

A rock chip is small damage — but it doesn't stay small. Left alone, temperature swings, road vibration, and pressure from the frame can turn a dime-sized chip into a crack that runs the width of your windshield. Caught early, most chips can be repaired in about 30 minutes, right where your vehicle is parked.

Repair preserves your original factory seal and windshield — no need for full replacement, and no need to recalibrate ADAS cameras and sensors the way a full replacement requires.

Common Questions

Rock Chip Repair FAQ

Will insurance cover it?

Most comprehensive auto insurance policies cover chip repair with little to no deductible. We can help you check your coverage before booking.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are done in about 30 minutes, on-site, while you wait.

Can any chip be repaired?

Most chips smaller than a quarter, away from the edge of the glass, are repairable. If damage has already spread into a long crack, replacement is usually the safer option — we'll tell you straight if that's the case.

What's your guarantee?

If a repaired chip fails and spreads, we stand behind the work — talk to us about your options, including credit toward a full replacement.
